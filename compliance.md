# Compliance and authorization boundaries

This Skill is for research using publicly accessible business information. Public availability does not remove obligations imposed by website terms, robots directives, privacy law, marketing law, database rights, or the user's own policies.

## Allowed by default

- Search public web pages and public business directories.
- Record company-level facts relevant to B2B qualification.
- Record public business contact channels when they appear on an official or credible source.
- Create local research tables, evidence files, and coverage reports.
- Label missing, conflicting, stale, or uncertain information.

## Not authorized by this Skill

- Sending email, messages, connection requests, or form submissions.
- Uploading contacts to a CRM, ad audience, enrichment service, or marketing platform.
- Buying gated data or accepting new commercial terms.
- Logging in, solving or bypassing CAPTCHAs, evading rate limits, or circumventing access controls.
- Accessing private accounts, personal inboxes, secrets, or credentials.
- Inferring private email addresses, direct phone numbers, identity, demographics, or sensitive traits.
- Claiming consent, lawful basis, opt-in status, deliverability, or marketing eligibility without evidence and a separate compliance review.

Obtain separate explicit authorization before any outreach or external write. At that stage, review applicable jurisdiction, lawful basis, suppression lists, consent requirements, sender identity, and channel-specific rules.

## Data minimization

Collect only fields needed for the stated B2B research purpose. Prefer role-based business contacts such as `sales@`, `info@`, or an official contact form over personal contact details when either can satisfy the task.

For a named person, retain the name and job title only when a public source explicitly connects that person to the company and the role is relevant. Keep the source URL and observed date. Do not infer an email pattern for that person.

## Source access

Honor robots directives, terms, authentication boundaries, rate limits, and technical blocking. A blocked source is a coverage limitation, not permission to bypass the block. Record the source, failure mode, date, and any compliant alternative used.

When a provider's license or terms limit redistribution, store only the minimum derived facts and provenance permitted for the task. Do not copy entire protected directories into a distributable Skill.

## Truthfulness and uncertainty

Never invent a company, customer relationship, sales figure, ranking, purchase volume, contact, title, phone, or email. Do not convert correlation, a social post, or a directory category into confirmed purchasing intent.

Use:

- `已验证`: a credible source directly supports the field;
- `待验证`: a candidate value exists but lacks sufficient corroboration or has a conflict;
- `公开渠道未找到`: a reasonable public-source search found no evidence for the requested field.

Preserve conflicting values and cite both sources. Prefer the official and most recent source, but state the resolution as a reasoned choice rather than erasing the conflict.

## Retention and reporting

Store provenance with the record: source URL, source type, observation time, extraction method, and verification status. Avoid retaining irrelevant personal data. If deletion or retention requirements are provided, apply them to research artifacts and document what was removed.

Reports must distinguish local research completion from any online action. Never state or imply that outreach, consent verification, CRM import, or publication occurred unless it was separately authorized and verified.
